PLANNED SYSTEMS

Built around your actual dino.

The game server remains the source of truth while the website handles the companion features around it.

01

Dino Storage

Store and redeem dinos while preserving growth, mutations, Prime, food, thirst, diets, lineage and current skin.

02

Skin Collection

Community JSON skins, nested birth skins, regional hatching skins, random rewards and automatic skin restoration.

03

Market & Auctions

Buy blank dinos, trade stored dinos and run timed auctions with ownership locking to prevent duplication.

04

Events & Quests

Species-specific bonus events, quests, minigames, crates, tokens and limited rewards.

05

Navigation

See your own location, set destinations, use preset teleports and get landmark-based hunting activity reports.

06

Entomb & Lineage

Keep lineage history, preserve improved Entomb mutations and reapply the dino's saved skin to its fresh hatchling life.
